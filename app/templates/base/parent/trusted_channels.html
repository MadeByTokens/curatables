YouTube channels you trust, checked by their channel ID, so a look-alike channel with the same name doesn't count. Videos your kids ask for from these channels can go into the library without waiting for you, and a kid can be set to find only these channels on their Find new page. Every rule in Settings → Video requests (blocked words, length, no live streams) still applies. Video requests shows what was added without asking, and lets you take it back.

For the whole family

Off: kids search all of YouTube, through your rules. On: their searches show only videos and channels from their trusted channels. Each kid can be set differently (Kids → Edit).
