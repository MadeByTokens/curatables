Shutting down

The box is turning off.
  1. Wait until the green light on the box stops flickering (about 20 seconds). The red light may stay on; that is normal.
  2. Then it is safe to unplug it.
  3. To turn it on again, unplug the power for a few seconds and plug it back in.
