{# What a parent can do when the videos drive is filling up. #} {% set days = request.app.state.config.storage.cache_days %}
  • Use a bigger drive. Plug in a USB drive and press Use for videos in Box → Drives; the videos are copied to it.
  • Free up space. {% if days %}Videos nobody watched for {{ days }} days already lose their files; a shorter time frees more. {% else %}Remove the files of videos nobody watched for a while: they stay listed and download again when a kid taps them. {% endif %} Settings → Freeing up space
  • Pick what nobody watches. Content, least watched first shows how often each video was played and its size; tick some and press Free up files (they stay listed) or Delete.
  • Delete what you no longer need. The storage report shows which channels take the most space.
