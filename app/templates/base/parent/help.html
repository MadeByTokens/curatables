Help

Getting Started

  1. Organize into channels — Go to Channels and make a few shelves (e.g. "Animals", "Music", "Bedtime").
  2. Add each child — Go to Kids. Choose a name, avatar, optional PIN, theme, which channels they see, and what they're allowed to do. New kids start with watching, reactions, downloading and hiding.
  3. Add videos — Go to + Add and paste a video, channel or playlist link from YouTube or any other site yt-dlp supports. Watch each video in the preview, tick that you've seen it all, and confirm.
  4. Open the kid address on the tablet — {{ kid_address(request) }}, or scan the QR code on the Kids page. Add it to the home screen and open it from that icon, and use Guided Access (iPad) or App pinning (Android) to keep your child inside it. If the tablet says "This browser didn't keep the sign-in", the browser is blocking cookies: on an iPad or iPhone, use the home screen icon, which keeps its own sign-in; elsewhere, allow cookies in the browser's settings.

Key Concepts

Channels

Channels are shelves of videos. Each kid can see all of them, only the ones you tick, or none of yours (Kids → Edit → Which channels can they see?).

What each kid can do

  • PIN — optional, 4–6 digits or 3 pictures; keeps siblings out of each other's profiles (not a strong lock)
  • Theme — Default, Playful, Calm, or Big & clear (large text, high contrast)
  • Search — off, or search within the approved library only
  • Switches — reactions, comments, tags, customizing, uploads, automatic subtitles, downloading and hiding videos. Turn off what your child doesn't need: fewer buttons, fewer ways to get lost.

Downloads and disk space

Videos download when you add them, one at a time, and are converted so they play on old tablets; on a Raspberry Pi 4 that takes a few minutes per video. Content → Downloading shows progress; failed ones have a Retry button. A download waiting or running has Pause (Resume carries on from where it stopped) and Stop (the video stays listed, with Download again); Pause all and Resume all do every download at once.

Every video is kept until you delete it. If space runs short, turn on Freeing up space in Settings: a video nobody watched for the number of days you choose loses its file, stays listed, and downloads again the next time it's tapped. Videos marked Keep forever on their edit page are never touched. To choose yourself, sort Content by least watched, tick what nobody watches and press Free up files (they stay listed) or Delete. The dashboard warns you when the videos drive, or the box's own memory card, is getting full; downloads and uploads stop before either fills up.

Video requests

Switch on Ask for new videos for a kid (Kids → Edit) and they get a Find new page that searches YouTube through this server, showing only results that pass the rules in Settings → Video requests. What they ask for waits in Video requests (the oldest 100 at a time, with the 20 latest decisions below); nothing downloads until you add it. Remove and Not this one can be undone under Recently decided. They see the titles and pictures of the results, so keep the word list strict or let them find only YouTube channels you trust.

Trusted YouTube channels are matched by their channel ID, so a look-alike with the same name doesn't count. Trust a channel for every kid or for one; on a kid's page (Kids → Edit) you can untick a family channel for that kid or add one for them only. Requests from a kid's trusted channels can go straight into a channel you pick, up to 10 a day for each kid (you can change the number); after that they wait for you until the next day, marked "Waited: daily limit reached" (your kid isn't told about the limit). Anything added that way can be taken back with Remove from library. Whether kids find only their trusted channels, and whether those requests go straight in, are set for the whole family on the Trusted YouTube channels page; each kid can be set differently on their page.

Each kid's Home

Home is each kid's own screen, like a tablet's home screen. At first it shows their 12 newest videos. With Change my Home your kid puts a video in any of its 24 spots (+), moves one (Move, then the spot), or takes one off (✕); Put on my Home under a video adds it too. Once they change it, Home stays as they arranged it: new videos show under All and in their channels, not on Home by themselves.

Downloading and hiding videos

Download under a video saves it onto the kid's device (on an iPad or iPhone, into the Files app, under Downloads), named as your kid sees it. Switching it off removes the button and the browser's own download option; it keeps an honest kid from saving videos, but isn't a lock.

Hide under a video takes it out of that kid's lists, search and tags; other kids still see it. You see it under Needs your attention on the dashboard and under Content → Hidden by a kid. On the video's page, Delete video removes it for everyone, Keep it hidden leaves it hidden for that kid only, and Show it again puts it back in their lists.

Kid uploads

Videos a kid uploads into their own channel appear right away for that kid. Uploads into a shared channel wait for you under Needs your attention on the dashboard.

Comments & Reactions

Kids see comments from you and from family members who can see the same video. You can delete any comment from the video's page or from Stats.

Troubleshooting

Video won't download

  • Open Content → Failed; each video says what went wrong and what to try
  • YouTube changes often: update the downloader (Box → Updates on the Curatables box, Settings → Updates elsewhere), then Retry
  • Still blocked: Settings → Advanced → Anti-Bot → Browser Impersonation (makes downloads look like a normal browser)

Kid can't see a video

  • It must be fully downloaded (Content → Downloading shows the ones still going)
  • It must be in a channel that kid may see
  • It must not be hidden or waiting for your OK
  • The kid may have hidden it: see Content → Hidden by a kid

Kids see "the videos are resting"

The USB drive with the videos is unplugged or was not opened. Plug it in, then press Reconnect on Box → Drives & backups.

Updating Curatables

Box → Updates installs new versions, keeping all videos, kids and settings.

Something else is wrong

On the Curatables box, Box → Diagnostics checks the network, the videos drive, the power supply and more, and says what to do. It can also make a report to send to whoever helps you. Wi-Fi, USB drives and restarts are under Box.

Forgot the parent password

On the Curatables box: turn it off, put its SD card in any computer, create an empty file named curatables-reset.txt on it, and turn the box on again; setup opens so you can choose a new password. On another computer, see "I forgot the parent password" in the getting-started guide.

Data & Privacy

  • Everything stays on this server; nothing is sent to any cloud service
  • Kids' devices never connect to the video sites — videos play from this server
  • Library location: {{ request.app.state.config.storage.path }}
  • The database is backed up every night (permanent installs)

Feedback & helping out

Found a bug, or have an idea that would help your family? Tell us in a GitHub issue (a free GitHub account is all it takes). Curatables is open source, and help is welcome: fixing a typo, translating, testing on your hardware or writing code. See the project page to get started.

Thank you! For trusting Curatables with your family’s videos, and for every report and idea that makes it better.
