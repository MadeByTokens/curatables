Help

Getting Started

  1. Organize into channels — Go to Channels and make a few shelves (e.g. "Animals", "Music", "Bedtime").
  2. Add each child — Go to Kids. Choose a name, avatar, optional PIN, theme, which channels they see, and what they're allowed to do. New kids start with "watch + react" only.
  3. Add videos — Go to + Add and paste a video, channel or playlist link from YouTube or any other site yt-dlp supports. Watch each video in the preview, tick that you've seen it all, and confirm.
  4. Open the kid address on the tablet — {{ kid_address(request) }}, or scan the QR code on the Kids page. Add it to the home screen, and use Guided Access (iPad) or App pinning (Android) to keep your child inside it.

Key Concepts

Channels

Channels are shelves of videos. Each kid can be limited to some of them (Kids → Edit → Visible channels). A kid who is limited and has no channels ticked sees only their own channels — never "everything".

What each kid can do

  • PIN — optional, 4–6 digits or 3 pictures; keeps siblings out of each other's profiles (not a strong lock)
  • Theme — Default, Playful, Calm, or Big & clear (large text, high contrast)
  • Search — off, or search within the approved library only
  • Switches — reactions, comments, tags, customizing, uploads, automatic subtitles. Turn off what your child doesn't need: fewer buttons, fewer ways to get lost.

Downloads and disk space

Videos download when you add them, one at a time, and are converted so they play on old tablets; on a Raspberry Pi 4 that takes a few minutes per video. Content → Downloading shows progress; failed ones have a Retry button.

A video nobody watched for the number of days set in Settings is removed from disk and downloads again the next time it's tapped. Mark favourites keep forever on their edit page. When the disk is full, the least recently watched videos are removed first.

Video requests

Switch on Ask for new videos for a kid (Kids → Edit) and they get a Find new page that searches YouTube through this server, showing only results that pass the rules in Settings → Video requests. What they ask for waits in Video requests; nothing downloads until you add it. They see the titles and pictures of the results, so keep the word list strict or allow only channels you trust.

Kid uploads

Videos a kid uploads into their own channel appear right away for that kid. Uploads into a shared channel wait for you under Needs your attention on the dashboard.

Comments & Reactions

Kids see comments from you and from family members who can see the same video. You can delete any comment from the video's page or from Stats.

Troubleshooting

Video won't download

  • Open Content → Failed; each video says what went wrong and what to try
  • YouTube changes often: Settings → Updates → Update yt-dlp now, then Retry
  • Still blocked: Settings → Advanced → Anti-Bot → Browser Impersonation

Kid can't see a video

  • It must be fully downloaded (Content → Downloading shows the ones still going)
  • It must be in a channel that kid may see
  • It must not be hidden or waiting for your OK

Something else is wrong

On the Curatables box, Box → Diagnostics checks the network, the videos drive, the power supply and more, and says what to do. It can also make a report to send to whoever helps you. Wi-Fi, USB drives and restarts are under Box.

Forgot the parent password

On the Curatables box: turn it off, put its SD card in any computer, create an empty file named curatables-reset.txt on it, and turn the box on again; setup opens so you can choose a new password. On another computer, see "I forgot the parent password" in docs/getting-started.md.

Data & Privacy

  • Everything stays on this server; nothing is sent to any cloud service
  • Kids' devices never connect to the video sites — videos play from this server
  • Library location: {{ request.app.state.config.storage.path }}
  • The database is backed up every night (permanent installs)
