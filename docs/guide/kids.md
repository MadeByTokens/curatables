# Curatables in pictures: for kids

A picture guide to the kids' screen, to look at together with your kid.
The captions are short, for reading aloud.

Each kid only sees the buttons a grown-up has switched on, so some of
these screens may not be on your kid's tablet. Making profiles and
switching things on is in the [guide for parents](parents.md). The
pictures come from a real box with three example kids, Mia, Leo and
Lucía. The videos are free films (see the
[credits](parents.md#credits)).

## Who's watching?

**Tap your picture.**

![Who's watching? Leo, Lucía and Mia](img/kid-who-is-watching.webp)

**Some kids have a secret.** Tap your three pictures in the right order.

![Mia taps her secret pictures: dog, frog, then one more](img/kid-picture-pin.webp)

Or tap your numbers, then ✓. Got it wrong? Just try again.

![Lucía's number keypad, saying the numbers were wrong, in Spanish](img/kid-number-pin.webp)

## Home

**Home shows all your videos.** Tap a channel at the top to see only
those. **Tap a picture to watch it.**

![Mia's home with six videos](img/kid-home.webp)

## Watching a video

**Tap a face to say how you liked it.** You can put a word on the video
to find it again, and write a comment. A grown-up may write back.

![Watching Caminandes 3: faces, a tag and a comment](img/kid-watch.webp)

**Stopped in the middle?** Next time, tap **Carry on** to go on from
there, or **From the start** to watch it all again.

![Carry on or From the start](img/kid-carry-on.webp)

**At the end**, tap **Again** to watch it once more, or **Home** to pick
another one.

![The end of a video: Again or Home](img/kid-end.webp)

## Looking for a video

**Search** looks through your videos. Type a word and tap Search.

![Searching for "moon"](img/kid-search.webp)

**Tags** shows the words you put on videos. Tap one to see its videos.

![Mia's tags: llama](img/kid-tags.webp)

## Asking for a new video

**Find new** looks on YouTube. **Tap Ask** on a video you like. A
grown-up watches it first. "Already here" means you have it.

![Find new: looking for Caminandes](img/kid-find-new.webp)

You can give it a name, say what it is about and pick a channel. Or just
tap **Send**.

![Mia names her video Llama Drama and picks Cartoons](img/kid-ask.webp)

**What I asked for** shows how it's going. When a grown-up says yes, it
says **Yes! Watch**.

![What I asked for: Yes! Watch](img/kid-asked.webp)

## Your own videos

**Upload** sends a video from the tablet. You can make your own channel
for it first.

![Uploading My drawing to Mia's Films](img/kid-upload.webp)

**Customize** on a video gives it your own name, words and picture. Only
you see them.

![Customize a video](img/kid-customize.webp)

## Help

**Lost? Tap Help.** It shows what each button does. Home always takes you
back to the start.

![The Help page](img/kid-help.webp)

## Other looks and languages

A grown-up can choose a different look and language for each kid. Leo's
phone has the **Big & clear** look in Portuguese and only shows him
cartoons.

![Leo's phone: Big & clear, in Portuguese](img/kid-phone-big-and-clear.webp)

Lucía's tablet has the **Calm** look in Spanish.

![Lucía's tablet: Calm, in Spanish](img/kid-tablet-calm.webp)
